# Barbara Liskov

MIT Institute Professor. Turing Award recipient (2008). Pioneer of data abstraction and subtyping.

## Books in This List
- [[books/Program Development in Java]]

## Key Contributions
- Liskov Substitution Principle (the "L" in [[concepts/SOLID Principles|SOLID]])
- CLU language (iterators, exceptions, generics — later adopted by Java, C++, Python)
- Data abstraction as organizing principle
- Argus (distributed computing language)

## Connections
- [[authors/Robert C Martin|Martin]] popularized LSP to practitioners as part of SOLID
- [[authors/Gang of Four|GoF]] patterns implicitly rely on behavioral subtyping
