# Ward Cunningham

Inventor of the wiki. Agile Manifesto co-author. Patterns community pioneer.

## Key Contributions
- Invented the wiki (WikiWikiWeb, 1995)
- Coined "technical debt" metaphor (1992)
- Co-invented CRC Cards with [[authors/Kent Beck|Kent Beck]]
- Portland Pattern Repository (online home of patterns community)
- FIT (Framework for Integrated Test)
- "The simplest thing that could possibly work"

## Connections
- CRC Cards with [[authors/Kent Beck|Beck]]
- Portland Pattern Repository hosted [[authors/Gang of Four|GoF]] discussions
- [[concepts/Technical Debt]] metaphor heavily used by [[authors/Martin Fowler|Fowler]]
