# Gang of Four (GoF)

Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, John Vlissides. Authors of the seminal design patterns book.

## Books in This List

- [[books/Design Patterns]]

## Key Contributions

- 23 classic [[concepts/Design Patterns|design patterns]]
- "Program to an interface, not an implementation"
- "Favor composition over inheritance"
- Erich Gamma co-created JUnit with [[authors/Kent Beck|Beck]]

## Connections

- [[authors/Grady Booch|Booch]] wrote the foreword
- Inspired by [[authors/Christopher Alexander|Christopher Alexander]]'s *A Pattern Language*
- Patterns community grew from [[authors/Ward Cunningham|Cunningham]]'s Portland Pattern Repository
- [[authors/Martin Fowler|Fowler]] extended patterns to enterprise (PoEAA)
