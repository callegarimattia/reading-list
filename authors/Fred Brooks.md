# Fred Brooks

IBM System/360 project manager. Professor at UNC Chapel Hill. Turing Award recipient (1999).

## Books in This List
- [[books/The Mythical Man-Month]]
- [[books/The Design of Design]]

## Key Contributions
- Brooks's Law ("adding manpower to a late project makes it later")
- "No Silver Bullet" — essential vs. accidental complexity
- Second-system effect
- Managed IBM System/360 and OS/360

## Connections
- Essential vs. accidental complexity adopted by [[authors/Eric Evans|Evans]] in DDD
- Intellectual foundation that software is inherently hard — every later author builds on this
