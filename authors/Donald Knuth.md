# Donald Knuth

Professor Emeritus at Stanford. Turing Award recipient (1974). "Father of the analysis of algorithms."

## Books in This List

- [[books/The Art of Computer Programming]]

## Key Contributions

- Rigorous algorithm analysis (Big O popularization)
- Literate programming (programs as literature)
- TeX typesetting system
- "Premature optimization is the root of all evil"

## Connections

- Contemporary of [[authors/Edsger Dijkstra|Dijkstra]]; nuanced response to GOTO paper
