# SOLID Principles

Five OO design principles articulated by [[authors/Robert C Martin|Robert C. Martin]]:

- **S**ingle Responsibility Principle — a class has one reason to change
- **O**pen-Closed Principle — open for extension, closed for modification
- **L**iskov Substitution Principle — subtypes must be substitutable for their base types (from [[authors/Barbara Liskov|Barbara Liskov]])
- **I**nterface Segregation Principle — no client should depend on methods it doesn't use
- **D**ependency Inversion Principle — depend on abstractions, not concretions

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Foundation of [[concepts/Clean Architecture]]
