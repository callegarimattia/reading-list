# Object-Oriented Design

Organizing software around objects that encapsulate data and behavior. The dominant paradigm from the 1990s onward.

## Core Ideas

- Encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism
- "Program to an interface, not an implementation" (GoF)
- "Favor composition over inheritance" (GoF)
- [[concepts/SOLID Principles]] as OO design guidelines
- [[concepts/Design Patterns]] as reusable OO solutions

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- [[concepts/SOLID Principles]] guide OO design
- [[concepts/Design Patterns]] catalog OO solutions
- [[concepts/Functional Programming]] is the alternative paradigm
- [[concepts/Domain-Driven Design]] tactical patterns are OO patterns
