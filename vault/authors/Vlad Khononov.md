# Vlad Khononov

Software engineer and author focused on domain-driven design and software complexity. Known for his work on coupling analysis and DDD in practice.

- Nuanced coupling taxonomy beyond simple loose/tight dichotomy
- Practical frameworks for reasoning about design trade-offs through coupling analysis
