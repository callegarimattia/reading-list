# Sandi Metz

Software developer, teacher, and author. Known for making object-oriented design practical and accessible. Renowned Ruby community figure.

## Key Contributions

- Practical approach to [[concepts/Object-Oriented Design]]
- [[concepts/SOLID Principles]] made accessible through concrete examples
- Dependency management and cost-of-change thinking
