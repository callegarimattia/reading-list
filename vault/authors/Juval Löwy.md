# Juval Löwy

Software architect and consultant. Founder of IDesign. Known for his method of system decomposition based on volatility rather than functionality.

## Books in This List

- [[books/Righting Software]]

## Key Contributions

- Volatility-based decomposition method
- The Method (IDesign architecture method)
- Project design for estimation and staffing

## Connections

- Offers alternative decomposition to [[authors/Eric Evans|Evans]]' domain-centric approach in [[books/Domain-Driven Design]]
- Complements [[authors/Robert C Martin|Martin]]'s architecture boundaries in [[books/Clean Architecture]]
- Service decomposition contrasts with [[authors/Sam Newman|Newman]]'s [[books/Building Microservices]]
