# Acceptance Testing

Tests that verify a system satisfies its business requirements. In a [[concepts/Behavior-Driven Development|BDD]] context, these are derived directly from the scenarios written collaboratively with stakeholders.

## Core Ideas

- Tests written from the user/business perspective
- Verify the *what*, not the *how*
- Often automated using Given-When-Then format
- Serve as both validation and [[concepts/Living Documentation|documentation]]

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Output of [[concepts/Behavior-Driven Development]] and [[concepts/Specification by Example]]
- Distinct from unit/integration tests (higher level, business-facing)
- Part of [[concepts/Living Documentation]] when kept green and up to date
