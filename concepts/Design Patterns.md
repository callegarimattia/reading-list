# Design Patterns

Reusable solutions to commonly occurring problems in software design. Named, cataloged, and organized by intent (creational, structural, behavioral).

## Core Ideas

- Patterns are not invented, they are discovered in recurring practice
- "Program to an interface, not an implementation"
- "Favor composition over inheritance"
- Originated from [[authors/Christopher Alexander|Christopher Alexander]]'s architectural patterns
- Multiple levels: code-level, enterprise, domain, architectural

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- Foundation for [[concepts/Object-Oriented Design]]
- [[concepts/Domain-Driven Design]] tactical patterns (Entity, Value Object, Aggregate) are domain patterns
- [[concepts/Software Architecture]] patterns operate at a higher level
- [[concepts/Refactoring]] toward patterns
