# Three Amigos

A discovery workshop format where three perspectives — business, development, and testing — collaboratively explore a feature using concrete examples before development begins.

## Core Ideas
- Business (what do we need?), Dev (how do we build it?), Test (what could go wrong?)
- Use examples to surface misunderstandings early
- Output: agreed-upon scenarios in Given-When-Then
- Short, focused sessions (not long meetings)
