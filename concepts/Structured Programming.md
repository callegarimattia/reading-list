# Structured Programming

A programming paradigm advocated by [[authors/Edsger Dijkstra|Dijkstra]] that eliminates GOTO in favor of structured control flow: sequence, selection (if/else), and iteration (loops).

## Core Ideas
- Eliminate GOTO — use structured control flow
- Programs can be reasoned about mathematically
- Separation of concerns (coined by Dijkstra)
- Formal correctness proofs

## Relationship to Other Concepts
- Precursor to [[concepts/Object-Oriented Design]] and [[concepts/Functional Programming]]
- Dijkstra's THE system layers prefigure [[concepts/Software Architecture]] layering

## Books Covering This
- [[books/A Discipline of Programming]] — Dijkstra's formal treatment
